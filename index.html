About Us

Serving Kinniya Zone with Dedication

The Kinniya Zonal Education Office functions under the Eastern Provincial Ministry of Education, overseeing all primary and secondary schools in the Kinniya educational zone in the Trincomalee District.

Our office is committed to improving educational standards, implementing national curriculum, supporting teachers, and ensuring equitable access to quality education for every child in the zone regardless of language, religion, or background.

We coordinate with schools, parents, and the community to create a nurturing learning environment that prepares students to meet national and global challenges.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

🔭

Our Vision

To be a model zonal education office that nurtures a generation of educated, creative, and responsible citizens who contribute meaningfully to the progress of Sri Lanka and the global community — through inclusive, equitable, and quality education.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide effective and efficient administrative support to all schools in the Kinniya Zone, enabling every student to realize their full potential through access to quality teaching, modern learning resources, and safe, supportive educational environments.

What We Offer

Our Services

Comprehensive administrative and educational support for all schools in the zone

📝

School Registration

New student enrolment processing and school admission coordination for all grade levels.

👩‍🏫

Teacher Transfers

Processing of teacher transfer requests, promotions, and inter-zonal postings.

📊

Academic Supervision

Regular school inspections, curriculum supervision, and academic performance monitoring.

🎓

Examinations

Coordination of Grade 5 Scholarship, O/L and A/L examinations within the zone.

💰

School Grants

Distribution of government school development grants and scholarship funds to eligible students.

🏗️

Infrastructure

Overseeing construction, renovation, and maintenance of school buildings and facilities.

📚

Textbooks & Supplies

Coordination of free textbook distribution and school supply allocation each academic year.

🩺

School Health

School health programs, nutrition monitoring, and welfare support for vulnerable students.
